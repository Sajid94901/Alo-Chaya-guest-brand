Compliance & Legal Foundation

All pay structures and staffing policies are aligned with Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 and amendments.

  • Minimum Base Pay per rank respects statutory minimum wage thresholds (≥12,000 BDT where applicable).
  • Gross Pay = Base Pay (BE + FB) + Allowances (housing, medical, transport).
  • Metal Handshake Tiers (Platinum → Iron) align BE + FB to legal and merit-based standards.
  • Staffing levels, phase-based scenarios, and span-of-control ensure operational compliance without exceeding legal caps.
  • Employee rights for overtime, weekly holidays, and benefits fully respected per Sections 3–7 of the Act.
  • Applies to all operational, culinary, and housekeeping personnel.
  • Periodic review ensures adjustments remain compliant with updates from the Minimum Wage Board.

Sources: Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, Minimum Wage Board Notifications, statutory amendments.
