HR Policies, Principles & Doctrine

  • Core Philosophy: “Dignified Livelihoods & Holistic Well-Being” – Dignity wages, predictable payroll, staff wellness, clear career paths.
  • Hiring & Assessment: Multi-stage selection with Maslow-informed screening, 3-month probation, and role simulations.
  • Leadership Model Suite: Jim Collins Level 5, John C. Maxwell 5 Levels, Kelley Followership, Situational Leadership.
  • Personality & Psychometrics: NEO Big Five, DeYoung & Peterson facets for senior roles, baseline + role-performance validation.
  • Assessment & Promotion: Short assessment centers, leaderless group tasks, operational case studies, promotions gated by merit.
  • Governance & Brand Alignment: HR metrics mapped to Brand Identity & Brand Performance; staff are live brand ambassadors.
  • Project Lead Contingency: Project lead contributes 50% of salary to equity pool until breakeven KPI met.
