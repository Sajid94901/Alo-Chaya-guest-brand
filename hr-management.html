The Doctrine of Harmony

We do not use linear "Annual Reviews." We use the Boyd Cycle (OODA Loop) applied to HR.

  • Observe: Daily "Why Mirror" & Floor Efficiency Data.
  • Orient: Compare against the ACEM Score (Physique) and GLSM (Intellect).
  • Decide: Assign "Kata" (Training) or Promotion.
  • Act: Execute the role (e.g., Project Lead executes 250k strategy).

The Academy Cycle

1 Identification

Identify Gap in ACEM Score (e.g., Staff has low "Physique").

2 Design

Create specific "Kata" (Drill) in the Daily Schedule.

3 Delivery

Project Lead or Exec Chef teaches the skill (On-the-Job).

4 Evaluation

Re-score ACEM after 30 days (Shokugeki Challenge).

The Performance Cycle

1 Planning

Set GLSM/ACEM Targets for the Quarter.

2 Monitoring

Daily Stand-up & "Why Mirror" Debriefs.

3 Developing (Versatility)

Cross-Training. Moving from Specialist to Generalist to Versatilist.

4 Rewarding

Unlock the 88k Dignity Wage based on Score.
