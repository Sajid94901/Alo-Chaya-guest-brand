Provision Bureau — Finance & Resource Allocation

Budget & Forecast

Daily — cash-flow check; treasury vs operations ledger; alert if variance > 0.5%.

Weekly — rolling 4-week forecast; Responsible: Financial Analyst (D); Accountable: Bureau Chief Provision (A); Owner: Rank 2 Marquess (O).

Quarterly — variance analysis; Rank 1 Archduke reviews; DMADOV gate for capital projects.

D-R-A-S-C-I-O mapping

  • Drive (D): Financial Analyst / Ops Lead
  • Responsible (R): Accounts Officer
  • Accountable (A): Bureau Chief Provision (Rank 3)
  • Supporting (S): Auditor, Payroll Officer
  • Consulted (C): Ops Head, HR Lead
  • Informed (I): Rank 1 & Board
  • Owner (O): Rank 2 Marquess

  • GURABA virtue check: Al-Amin compliance required for vendor acceptance.

Daily invoice validation against PO. Any non-bank payment flagged as non-compliant. Responsible: Accounts Officer (R). Accountable: Bureau Chief Provision (A).

Monthly reconciliation with auditors; decertification triggers: missing voucher, cash > threshold, Section 55 violation.

Procurement & Vendor Alliances

Vendor scorecard: On-Time Delivery, Quality, Price Stability, Ethical Fit. Responsible: Procurement Lead (D). Accountable: Rank 2 (A). Owner: Archduke (O).

Quarterly vendor review; add 1 strategic vendor per quarter to reduce cost by 2–3% target.

Innovation & ROI

Audit, Compliance & DMADOV

All capital commits require Rank 1 signatory + documented quorum. DMADOV alert triggers on bypass attempts. Audit log immutable; flagged entries require Bureau Chief remediation within 72 hours.

Testing sequence: written hygiene exam → situational clean-up test → STAR practical (guest scenario) → Shokugiki-style timed cook-service if F&B crossover needed.
